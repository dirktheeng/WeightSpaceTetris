Tetris in weight space

Every rule of this game is in hand-written weights and biases. The page only runs the network once per tick, feeds it your keys and a random number, and draws the screen it returns.

Score: the green column of 16 cells beside the board is the network's binary count of cleared lines, high bit on top.
